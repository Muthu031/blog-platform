# Palindrome Linked List

Difficulty: Easy

Description:
Given the head of a singly linked list, determine if the linked list is a palindrome.

Input: `head = [1,2,2,1]`

Output: `true`

Hints:
- Find the middle of the list, reverse the second half, and compare the two halves.
- Optionally restore the list after comparison. Aim for O(n) time and O(1) extra space.

Tags: Linked List, Two Pointers
